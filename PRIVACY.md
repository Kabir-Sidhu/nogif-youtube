# Privacy Policy for NoGIF

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

NoGIF is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed to run entirely locally within your browser.

## Data Collection and Transmission
- **No Personal Data Collected:** NoGIF does not collect, track, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information, account credentials, browsing history, or user activity.
- **No External Servers:** NoGIF operates 100% on the client side. No data is sent to external servers, APIs, analytics providers, or third parties.
- **Local Settings Storage:** The extension uses `chrome.storage.local` (or `chrome.storage.sync` when available) solely to save your local UI preferences (filter mode, badge toggle) and comment statistics. This data never leaves your device or browser sync account.
- **Website Access:** The extension runs exclusively on `youtube.com` domains to detect and manage comment GIF elements. It does not monitor, read, or alter any other websites or browsing sessions.

## Third-Party Services
NoGIF does not use cookies, trackers, advertising networks, or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Changes to this Policy
If any future updates change how the extension functions, this document will be updated accordingly.
